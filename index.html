Strength Program · 3 Days a Week

Stacy Prego
Workout Program

Two lifting days and one fun movement day. Every exercise has 2 working sets. Setup gets you in position and Cue is what to think about mid-set. Push close to hard on each set, but stop if anything feels off.

Day 1

6 EXERCISES

01

Machine Shoulder Press

2 Sets · 8–12

Setup

Set the seat height so that when you're pressing, your hands are starting just above your shoulders, so that way you can push fully from your shoulder up and get a full range of motion. Make sure you sit upright, don't slouch, and have your feet planted in the ground. Your wrists should ideally be stacked over your elbows to avoid any kind of shearing tension on your joints.

Cue

Think about keeping your elbows in front of you while you're pressing (don't let them flare), and think about pushing up and back, instead of just up or up + forward.

02

Seated Cable Row

2 Sets · 10–15

Setup

The main thing here is the attachment. I'd like you to use two fabric D handles. You've probably seen the metal V grips and those are fine, but if you use the fabric D handles, when you eventually have a belly, the attachment won't get interrupted by the belly. Sit upright and grab the handles, and remember how you are when you're upright. I don't want you going anywhere behind that in terms of leaning back, you can lean forward slightly.

Cue

Grab the handles, and then I want you to pull pretty narrow, thinking about pulling your elbows back, not your wrists. With this, stop pretty short, stop about when your elbows are at 90° or a little bit more behind (the lats don't have any leverage past that point, so it's unnecessary fatigue).

03

Lateral Raise (Any Variant)

2 Sets · 6–12

Setup

You know how to do a lateral raise. You could do a cable, dumbbell, or a machine if they have one that feels good. I personally don't care, as long as you're doing a lateral raise that you don't dread

Cue

Think about raising your elbows in a circle, and try not to shrug your shoulders up as it gets hard. Try to keep it strictly using your shoulders, and once you feel yourself shrugging to get the weight up, call it there.

04

Any Squat Pattern (Leg Press, Smith Machine Squat, etc.)

2 Sets · 8–12

Setup

Like with the lateral raise, I don't care which one of these you choose to do, just pick one that you think you'll enjoy and stay consistent with it. As long as it is a squat pattern type machine or exercise, I'm happy

Cue

There's three things I want you to be conscious of when you're doing these

  1. Try to push through the middle of your foot. If you're coming up on your toes, then your feet are too close to you and you need to move them away, and vice versa if it's going through your heels
  2. Try to get as much knee flexion as possible. The more knee flexion the better, and that makes it more of a quad exercise, instead of being glutes or hips
  3. BREATHE!!!! Don't need you passing out, so breathe and if you're feeling light headed, stop.
05

Seated or Standing Hamstring Curl

2 Sets · 6–12

Setup

You know how to do a seated hamstring curl. A standing hamstring curl is another option (depends on if your gym has it or not) which I think you would enjoy more, but it's dependent on if you have access to that kind of machine. I'll wait to make a decision on this until we check out the gym

Cue

Going to wait on the cue until we know what your gym has

06

Hip Abductions

2 Sets · 10–15

Setup

Pretty simple setup since the machine does all of the setup for you practically. The only thing you MIGHT be able to adjust on the machine is the angle of the back pad. If you can, then make it so you're not fully upright, but slightly leaned back.

Cue

Think about opening your hips up, not just pushing your knees out. Initiate the movement from the hips and it'll feel harder, and you won't need to use as much weight compared to if you were going to just sweep your knees out

Day 2

6 EXERCISES

01

Single Leg B-Stance RDL

2 Sets · 8–12

Setup

These are a new one and one I think you'll enjoy. A bit hard putting into words what the setup is, so here is a great video ↗ which shows the setup. Follow this, and you'll be good

Cue

As usual, an RDL is entirely about pushing your hips BACK, not bending forward. Once you feel your hips stop moving back and you're starting to round your back to get lower, stop the movement and don't go any lower than that. Don't need you hurting your lower back and walking around like a question mark.

02

Any Pulldown Variation

2 Sets · 8–12

Setup

You know how to do a pulldown. When I say any pulldown variation, it could be a machine, it could be a cable, it could be assisted pull ups, doesn't matter. Find one you enjoy which consists of your arms going above your head and you pulling down, and stick with it

Cue

Think about pulling your elbows down to your hips, rather than pulling from your wrists. If pulling with both hands at once, have them slightly outside shoulder width. Single arm is also a possibility, but this has different cues so we'll see what you prefer.

03

Leg Extensions

2 Sets · 6–12

Setup

You know how to do these. The one thing I'll say is you can make a decision between doing both legs at once, or doing one leg at a time.

Cue

Have the pad just above your ankles, put the seat back so you're leaning back a bit, and extend. When you're extending your legs, think about kicking the wall IN FRONT of you, don't try to kick the ceiling. You're kicking OUT, not up.

04

Machine Chest Press

2 Sets · 8–15

Setup

Set the seat so that the handles are near your mid-chest and such that when you hold onto the handles, there is a straight line from your wrist to your elbows. This protects your wrists and makes sure you're not injuring yourself. Pull your shoulder blades back like you're holding a pencil between them and keep them like that for the entirety of the set.

Cue

Think about trying to push your biceps towards each other, instead of just pushing out. Don't round your back too much, and breathe (don't need you passing out).

05

Face Pull

2 Sets · 10–15

Setup

Have the cable slightly above where your head is when you're standing tall. You can use a rope or a straight bar to do these, but I would suggest using a rope. Grab the rope, squat down a little bit and spread your feet to make sure you're stable (like a table), and then do the face pulls.

Cue

You know how a double bicep flex looks? Of course you do, I hit those all the time in Asia. When you're doing the face pull, think about pulling your arms back as if you're gonna do a double bicep flex, this'll cue your rear delts to do the work instead of your mid back/traps, and it'll probably be a bit more difficult than how you've been doing them.

06

Hip Adductions

2 Sets · 10–15

Setup

Same setup as abductions, seat slightly back from fully upright so you're slightly leaned back

Cue

Think about closing your hips and bringing your inner thighs together, not your knees. Again, it'll feel harder this way and you won't need to use as much weight

Day 3

Fun Pregnancy Day

STRETCHES

This day is all about pregnancy, yay! I want you to have fun and do the fun pregnancy stretch things you see on instagram. There's really no format here, just getting your body moving for a dedicated third day in the week is a MASSIVE win, especially if you're able to do the two lifts earlier in the week. Pat yourself on the back, don't beat yourself up, and do some of the fun stuff you find.

01

Fun Pregnancy Stretches or Anything Else You Wanna Do

You can do as many or as little of these stretches as you want. Today's about you, you're doing great even being here in the gym, so just do things that you want. If you can aim for like 4-5 GREAT stretches, that's perfect and you're killing it.
